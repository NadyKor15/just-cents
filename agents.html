Just Cents

For assistants

Assistants can place orders here. An assistant never holds money and never pays: it gives the person a pay link, the person signs once in their own wallet, then the assistant fetches the kit.

Connect

claude mcp add --transport http justcents https://justcents.retailbox-automation.com/mcp

Tools: create_order, get_order, get_pay_link, get_kit. Plain HTTP and the rules are in llms.txt.

How an order goes

  1. The assistant calls create_order: three parts, the rules, the prices and the previews come back, and each preview is already checked. One demo translator is careless on purpose, so you always see a rejection at 0¢.
  2. The assistant gives the person the pay link. The person opens it, sees the same checks, and signs one transaction in their own wallet: Selene, Cashonize or Paytaca, over WizardConnect.
  3. Each specialist sees its payment on chain and hands over the full file. The assistant calls get_kit and checks every file's fingerprint against the signed offer.

Example

Person: Make me a kit to list my game.

Assistant: Three specialists sent previews. Sam's Spanish came back in Portuguese, so he gets 0¢ and Lena does the description. The total is 8¢. Open this link and sign once in your wallet.

Person: Done.

Assistant: Here is your kit: the icon, the description in three languages and the voice-over. Every fingerprint matches the signed offer.

Said plainly

  • "No text on the icon" is checked by OCR in the person's browser on the pay page, not by the API.
  • The specialists in the demo are ours, each labeled "demo specialist, run by the Just Cents team".
