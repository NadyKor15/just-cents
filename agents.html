Just Cents

For assistants

Assistants can place orders here. An assistant never holds money and never pays: it gives the person a pay link, the person signs once in their own wallet, then the assistant fetches the kit.

Connect

claude mcp add --transport http justcents https://justcents.retailbox-automation.com/mcp

Tools: create_order, get_order, get_pay_link, get_kit. Plain HTTP and the rules are in llms.txt.

How an order goes

  1. The assistant calls create_order: three parts, the rules, the prices and the previews come back, and each preview is already checked. One demo subtitles specialist is careless on purpose, so you always see a rejection at 0¢.
  2. The assistant gives the person the pay link. The person opens it, sees the same checks, and signs one transaction in their own wallet over WizardConnect (tested with Cashonize and Selene).
  3. Each specialist sees its payment on chain and hands over the full file. The assistant calls get_kit and checks every file's fingerprint against the signed offer.

Example

Person: Make me a kit to list my game.

Assistant: Three specialists sent previews. Sam's subtitles start 1.1 s after the voice, so he gets 0¢ and Lena's are used. The cover has two variants, A or B: which one? The total is 5¢. Open this link and sign once in your wallet.

Person: A. Done.

Assistant: Here they are: the trailer subtitles, cover A and the logo. Every fingerprint matches the signed offer.

Said plainly

  • The API checks what each specialist declares in its signed offer; the cover's sharpness and the logo's likeness to the sketch are measured on the full files, in the person's browser, after payment.
  • Each demo specialist names what made its file: Deepgram for the subtitles, Stability AI for the cover, the open-source VTracer for the logo.
  • The specialists in the demo are ours, each labeled "demo specialist, run by the Just Cents team".
