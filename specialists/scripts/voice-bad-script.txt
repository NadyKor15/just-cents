You are thirteen. It is summer in Dubai, a concert ticket costs three hundred and fifty dirhams, and your phone stays at the camp gate. So your sister Sara sets up Autopilot for you, an agent that runs your money. It does everything you write, word for word. On Tuesday and Thursday you are on a hike with no signal, and the agent decides on its own by your rules. At the weekend, the week statement shows which rule fired, where the money went, and how your summer will end. Then change one word in your rule and replay the week. Autopilot: Summer Rules. Your A I. Your rules. Your money.
