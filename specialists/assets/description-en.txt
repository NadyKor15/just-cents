Autopilot: Summer Rules is a free browser game where an agent handles your money by your rules, and is no smarter than they are. You are 13, it is summer in Dubai, and a concert ticket costs AED 350. Phones stay at the camp gate, so your sister Sara sets up Autopilot for you. Earn money in mini-games on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. On hike days there is no signal, and only your rules decide. At the weekend, a statement shows which rule fired and where the money went. Change one word, replay the week, and see what happens. Plays in English and Arabic on phone, tablet and laptop.
