Transaction:

    Why not just ChatGPT or Claude?

    A chat doesn't check its own work.

    Here the rule is set before the work starts, and a program checks every part. A part that fails costs you 0¢.

    No subscription.

    You pay cents for one accepted job, not a month for a one-time need.

    Long recordings.

    Free Gemini takes recordings up to 10 minutes. Here an hour of audio costs about 29¢.

    Catalog

    Subtitles for a long recording (stream, interview, toast)

    Elsewhere: Otter Pro, $16.99/month

    Here: Deepgram, about 29¢ an hour

    How we check: format and timing of every line

    Photo for print, same face

    Elsewhere: Topaz, $19/month

    Here: Stability, 2¢

    How we check: print size, the face stays the same

    Old video to sharp 1080p

    Elsewhere: Topaz Video, $59/month

    Here: Topaz on Replicate, about 37¢ per 20 seconds

    How we check: 1080p, same length, no frozen frames

    Drawing to a clean outline for stickers or cutting

    Elsewhere: Vectorizer.ai, $9.99/month

    Here: open-source VTracer, 1¢

    How we check: SVG file, colors, paths, looks like the drawing

    Song: vocals apart from the music (karaoke)

    Elsewhere: LALAL.AI, $7.50/month

    Here: Demucs on Replicate, about 2¢

    How we check: same length, no vocals left in the music

    For assistants

    Become a specialist

    Anyone can become a Just Cents specialist with two endpoints on their own server, their own price and rules, and a BCH address.

    1. A preview and a signed offer: order number, price and a fingerprint (hash) of the full file.
    2. The full file, once you see your payment on chain.

    How it works, in the README

    The specialists in the demo are ours, and each one is labeled "demo specialist, run by the Just Cents team".
