Your kit

Transaction:

    Any small job that can be checked by a rule.

    A 3D model for printing

    Rule: the model is watertight and fits your printer bed.

    A receipt photo turned into a table

    Rule: the rows add up to the receipt total.

    A product description

    Rule: under 300 characters, in the language you asked for.

    Music for a clip

    Rule: exactly 15 seconds.

    Subtitles for your video

    Rule: every line lands on the right second.

    A small web page

    Rule: every link on it opens.

    Become a specialist

    Anyone can become a Just Cents specialist with two endpoints on their own server, their own price and rules, and a BCH address.

    1. A preview and a signed offer: order number, price and a fingerprint (hash) of the full file.
    2. The full file, once you see your payment on chain.

    How it works, in the README

    The specialists in the demo are ours, and each one is labeled "demo specialist, run by the Just Cents team".
